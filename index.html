👆 Touch & Smartboard Gestures

  • One-Finger Drag: Rotates the 3D solid or pans the 2D graph view.
  • Pinch to Zoom: Scales the view in or out smoothly.
  • Zoom Buttons (+ / −): Tap on-screen zoom buttons for precise scaling without pinching.
  • Lock View (🔒): Freezes camera so you can interact or annotate without accidentally moving the graph.
  • Double Click / Tap: Instantly resets camera rotation and zoom level.
